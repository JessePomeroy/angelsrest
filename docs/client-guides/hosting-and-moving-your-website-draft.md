# Hosting your website and moving to another provider

**Draft for owner review. Proposed pricing is not an active fee schedule.**

Your website’s content is hosted on infrastructure I manage through Angels Rest. This includes the content you manage through your dashboard, such as photographs, page text, portfolio collections and product information. Hosting does not change ownership of the content you provide.

You can choose another hosting provider. If you decide to move, please contact me before your hosting ends so we can agree on what you need, the handoff date and any migration work.

## What happens when hosting ends

Your public website goes offline when your hosting service is offboarded, and your client dashboard access is disabled. Your content is normally retained for **90 days** after that date so a handoff can be arranged. Retention does not keep the public website online.

You can request earlier deletion. After the retention period, content is eligible for deletion; please arrange and download your handoff before that deadline rather than relying on continued storage. Payment, signed-document and other required business records may be retained separately. Existing paid-order and recipient-document services may need to remain available to complete outstanding commitments.

## Getting your content

I can provide one standard export of your available website content and stored media at no additional charge. Where applicable, this includes page text, portfolio information, product information and the image files held for your site. We will confirm the contents and delivery method before handoff.

A content export is a copy of your content, not a ready-to-run replacement website. Original files that were never uploaded cannot be included. Passwords, private service credentials, other clients’ data and Angels Rest’s shared infrastructure are not included. Website code and design deliverables are handled according to your project agreement.

The proposed basic export is an organized download containing structured content files, a media folder, an inventory and checksums. Current saved drafts are labeled separately from published content. It does not require purchasing migration assistance. Confidential CRM/customer-delivery records are handled as a separate, verified data-handoff request rather than mixed into a website-content download.

## Help with your move

I’m happy to help migrate your content to a new provider.

**Proposed standard migration assistance: $300 USD, including up to three hours of work.** We will agree on the tasks that fit within that allowance before work begins. Examples include organizing the handoff, transferring files to an agreed destination, coordinating with your new developer and checking the agreed transfer.

Additional work is **$100 USD per hour**, subject to an estimate and your approval before I exceed the included time. New-provider subscriptions, domain charges and other third-party costs are separate and require your approval.

Rebuilding the website, adapting its design to a different platform, replacing CMS/CRM features, migrating a complex shop or private-gallery system, and extensive data conversion require a separate quote. The shared Angels Rest dashboard and backend services do not automatically transfer to another host.

Before starting, I will confirm the scope, estimated cost, access needed, timing and how we will verify the handoff. Moving before hosting ends is the best way to plan around downtime; an uninterrupted transfer cannot be assumed without an agreed transition plan.
